I like problems where the slow answer is easy and the fast one takes real thought.

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Chennai Institute of Technology (2024–2028). Most of what I do comes down to data structures and algorithms: picking the right traversal, cutting the complexity, and making the code fit the hardware it has to run on.
